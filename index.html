Export 2 CSVs from QuickBooks Online

  1. Open invoices: In QuickBooks Online, open Reports and find Accounts Receivable Aging Detail (or your Open Invoices report). Set the report date to today.
  2. Export the aging report: Select Export, choose Excel, then save/export the data as CSV named Open_Invoices_Aging.csv.
  3. Recent bank deposits: Open your bank account register or transaction report, choose a recent date range (for example, the last 90 days), and export deposits as Recent_Bank_Deposits.csv.
  4. Check the files: Confirm the exports include invoice IDs, dates, open balances, deposit dates and amounts. Remove columns that are not needed.
Anonymization tip: In Excel, replace customer names in Column A with Client_001, Client_002, and so on before sharing. Keep invoice numbers, dates and amounts intact so matching still works.

QuickBooks menus can vary by plan and interface. Never send account credentials. Use a secure upload link for financial files; this page does not provide one.
